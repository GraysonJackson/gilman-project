I wanted to return to Ireland after my first visit in 2024. I remember standing at the Cliffs of Moher with my friends and walking the rainy streets of Galway. I loved the culture, the daily life, and the countryside. When I started looking into an internship abroad, Dublin was where I wanted to go.

From May 23 to August 15, 2026, I interned at NanobOx through World Endeavors. I am a computer science student at the University of Arkansas at Little Rock, and the internship counted toward my degree.

## Finding a program that fit

I heard about the Gilman Scholarship through Dr. Drew Smith at [UA Little Rock Study Abroad](https://ualr.edu/studyabroad/). I told him I wanted the chance to be in Ireland again and find something that fit my interests. He helped me choose World Endeavors, which had internship programs I could apply for.

World Endeavors helped me find a company and arranged my stay. I still had to interview for the placement myself. I ended up working at NanobOx's building in Dublin, where I built a web-based AI research assistant.

## Getting help with the application

Applying through World Endeavors involved talking with its representatives. For Gilman, I wrote essays about why I wanted to go. My peers in the Donaghey Scholars Program reviewed my writing, gave me feedback on grammar, and pushed me to explain more of what I wanted to say.

Their feedback helped me explain what I wanted from the experience more clearly.

The Donaghey Scholars Program also provided funding. Gilman helped pay for airfare and food. Without that support, I would not have been able to stay in Ireland as long as I did.

## Spending the summer away

Before leaving, I worried about missing my friends. I did miss them, but we stayed connected online. In Dublin, I also became friends with my housemates, who came from several different countries. I became especially close with two Irish housemates, and we talked about pretty much anything.

The summer gave me time to work, learn, and get to know people beyond a short visit. My routine included taking the public bus to work, cooking dinner, and spending time with people in the living room in the evenings.

I built this site to help other CS students see the opportunities available abroad and the connections they can form. If you are interested, [talk with UA Little Rock Study Abroad](https://ualr.edu/studyabroad/) about programs that fit your degree, and check the [Gilman application information](https://www.gilmanscholarship.org/applicants/applicants-faq-2/) for the current requirements.
