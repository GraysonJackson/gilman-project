When I first visited Ireland in 2024, I remember standing at the Cliffs of Moher with my friends and walking the rainy streets of Galway. Those memories were part of why I wanted to return. In 2026, I stayed from May 23 to August 15 for an internship in Dublin.

This time, I also had a daily routine. I lived in Phibsborough and took the public bus to work at NanobOx. In the evenings, I came home and cooked dinner every night.

## Living with people from different countries

My housemates were from Ireland, Germany, Italy, Greece, the Czech Republic, and Estonia. I became especially close with two Irish housemates. We talked about pretty much anything you could imagine.

Some evenings, we spent time together in the living room over games or drinks. We played a lot of Mario Party and Mario Kart, watched the World Cup together, and celebrated the Fourth of July for fun. For dinner, we often had curry or shepherd's pie.

I got to know people through the ordinary parts of sharing a house: cooking, coming back from work, and hanging out in the evenings.

Before the trip, I worried about being away from my friends for so long. I missed them, but we stayed connected online. I also made friends in the house where I was living.
