I am Grayson Jackson, a junior studying computer science at the University of Arkansas at Little Rock. I am active in the ACM Student Chapter and the Cybersecurity Club.

I went to Dublin through World Endeavors and interned at [NanobOx](https://www.nanobox.ie/) from May 23 to August 15, 2026. I built a web-based AI research assistant that answered questions using documents about nanobubbles. The internship counted toward my degree.

Gilman helped pay for airfare and food, and the Donaghey Scholars Program also provided funding. That support made it possible for me to stay as long as I did.

I built this site to help other CS students see the opportunities available abroad and the connections they can form. It is part of my Gilman follow-on service project.

If you want to ask me about studying or interning abroad, find me at a club meeting or message me on [Instagram]({{ site.instagram_url }}).
