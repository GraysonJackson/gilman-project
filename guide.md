## How to start

1. Talk to the study abroad office at your school. Ask what programs and internships they already support.
2. Check the Gilman site for eligibility and deadlines. Start early, because the essays take time.
3. Pick a program or internship that fits your degree plan so credits and timing work out.
4. Ask someone who has gone. Ask about the parts nobody puts in a brochure.
