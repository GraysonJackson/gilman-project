I am a computer science student at UA Little Rock. From May 23 to August 15, 2026, I interned at NanobOx in Dublin through World Endeavors. I built a web-based AI research assistant, and the internship counted toward my degree.

This guide describes what helped me arrange that experience. Your program, funding, and credit arrangements may be different, so start with your own university's study abroad office.

## Find a placement that fits your interests

I told Dr. Drew Smith at [UA Little Rock Study Abroad](https://ualr.edu/studyabroad/) that I wanted to return to Ireland and find something that fit me. He helped me choose World Endeavors, which offered internship programs.

World Endeavors helped me find a company and arranged my stay. I still had to interview for the placement myself. Ask a provider what is included, what you will arrange yourself, and how the placement process works.

I started planning in October. My interview with Dr. Ghaani was a discussion about the work I could do at NanobOx.

UA Little Rock lists its supported options on the [Study Abroad Programs page](https://ualr.edu/studyabroad/programs/).

## Ask about academic credit

My internship counted toward my CS degree through an internship course. Ask your department and the study abroad office how a placement would fit your degree plan and what needs to be approved before you go.

The work also gave me experience beyond class projects. I built an application from an idea and a small set of requirements, learned more about Ubuntu Server and AI tools, and saw how feedback affected my work.

## Look into funding

Gilman helped pay for my airfare and food. The Donaghey Scholars Program provided funding as well. Without that support, I would not have been able to stay as long as I did.

When planning your own experience, ask about program fees, housing, travel, food, local transport, and what any funding can cover.

## Have someone read your application

My peers in the Donaghey Scholars Program reviewed my writing for Gilman. They gave me grammar feedback and pushed me to explain more of what I wanted to say.

Use the [current Gilman application information](https://www.gilmanscholarship.org/applicants/applicants-faq-2/) for the essay requirements and ask someone you trust to review your draft.

## Ask about daily life too

My accommodation covered the room. I lived in Phibsborough, commuted by public bus, and cooked dinner every night. My housemates came from several countries, and I became friends with them through conversations and time together in the evenings.

Before leaving, I worried about missing my friends at home. I did miss them, but we stayed connected online. Ask where you would live, how you would commute, and how you would keep in touch with people at home.
