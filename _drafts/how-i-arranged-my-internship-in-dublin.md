I started planning my internship in October. I told Dr. Drew Smith at UA Little Rock that I wanted to return to Ireland and find a program that fit my interests. He helped me choose World Endeavors, which offered internship placements.

I spent May 23 to August 15, 2026, working at NanobOx, and the internship counted toward my computer science degree. Here is how I arranged that experience.

## Find a program that fits

I started with the [UA Little Rock Study Abroad office](https://ualr.edu/studyabroad/). I already knew I wanted to be in Ireland, but I needed help finding a program that matched what I wanted to do. World Endeavors had internship programs that fit.

Through the application process, I talked with World Endeavors representatives. They helped me find a company and arranged my stay. I still had to interview for the internship myself.

My interview with Dr. Ghaani was a conversation about what I could do at NanobOx. We discussed the work I could contribute, rather than following a long list of interview questions.

The accommodation arrangement covered my room. I cooked my own meals and used the public bus to commute.

Ask about that when you compare programs: what does the provider arrange, and what will you be responsible for? In my case, having help with the placement and housing did not remove the interview step.

## Apply for funding and get feedback

I learned about Gilman through Dr. Smith. Applying involved writing essays, and my peers in the Donaghey Scholars Program reviewed my work. They helped with grammar and pushed me to explain more of what I wanted to say.

Gilman helped pay for airfare and food, and the Donaghey Scholars Program provided funding too. Without that support, I could not have stayed as long as I did.

For your own application, use the [current Gilman requirements](https://www.gilmanscholarship.org/applicants/applicants-faq-2/). Ask someone you trust to read your writing and tell you where it needs more detail.

## Check how the internship fits your degree

My placement counted for academic credit through an internship course. Talk with your department and the study abroad office about how a program would fit your own degree plan before committing to it.

I do not have a specific change I would make to my application or preparation. What helped me was having an advisor to talk through the options, peers to review my writing, and funding that made the length of the stay possible.

If you are at UA Little Rock, [contact Study Abroad](https://ualr.edu/studyabroad/) to start that conversation. You can also ask me about my placement through ACM, Cybersecurity Club, or [Instagram]({{ site.instagram_url }}).
